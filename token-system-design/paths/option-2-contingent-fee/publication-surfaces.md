# Publication surfaces — what the website may say before counsel drafts

**Status: working note, not an offer and not a contract.** Companion to the [option-2 spec](README.md) and the [annex terms](annex-terms.md). Working notes: [2026-09-14 log](../../../docs/log/2026-09-14_website-soft-launch.md). This page is the paste source for public copy. It is **not** the Dutch *dienstenovereenkomst*. Counsel still owns that wording.

The live start-off is already written: hours → contribution weights `p` and a documented-effort ceiling `f`; cash only later, as a contingent invoice if a pool exists. An MoU that “there will be shares based on contribution,” or an informal profit split, is the [rejected labour quota](../../../docs/log/2026-09-10_no-investors-euro-shares.md). Skipping a DVB ruling is a [normal Belgian choice](../../../docs/log/2026-09-14_no-ruling-reserve-and-lookback.md). Skipping paper that matches the facts is not. FPS look at the facts whether or not a notary has seen them.

Investor cash stays **closed** on this soft-launch. The investor path may be named in outline on a **separate** page. It must not be offered. It must not share a page, a PDF, or a one-pager with the contributor framework ([2026-09-14 mistake](../../../docs/mistakes/2026-09-14_second-instrument-publishes-the-comparable.md); [ADR 2026-09-14 investor leg](../../../docs/decisions/2026-09-14_investor-leg-capped-participating-bond.md) point 6).

The two carve-outs in the [path map](../../README.md) (a contracted contributor’s own statement; an investor offering document) **do not apply to a website**. Public and promotional material stay under the ban.

**Prevention rules applied:** [no euro spot price on `p`](../../../docs/mistakes/2026-09-03_unit-price-on-growing-pool.md); [no day-one lifetime multiple](../../../docs/mistakes/2026-09-03_immediate-multiple-gold-rush.md); [no labour-quota cheap shares](../../../docs/mistakes/2026-09-04_cheap-share-euro-par.md); [a second instrument must not publish the comparable](../../../docs/mistakes/2026-09-14_second-instrument-publishes-the-comparable.md). Do not book a ceiling as money owed ([annex clause 27](annex-terms.md)).

## 1. Three layers

| Layer | Who sees it | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public website** | Anyone | One contributor page. Optional second page for capital, never combined. Terms are not final. Nothing is an offer or a promise to pay. |
| **Talk before join** | Named people | Same story as the contributor page. A few extra facts (company, independence, change, exit). Still no numbers. |
| **Trust group** | Named small group | Fact ledger of accepted work, plus a short contractor note. Not shares. Not a receivable. No invoices until a real pool exists. |

Do not freeze `k`, `r`, the mint curve, or pool policy on the site. Parameters and Dutch contract wording will be set with counsel. Until then, logged work is a **record of accepted contribution**, not a claim.

## 2. Two pages, never one document

**Page A — contributors.** How we intend to work with independent contributors.

**Page B — capital (optional, not open).** Money in is a separate instrument. This page is not an offer. Do not collect cash or “expressions of interest that lock a rate” through the site.

Never on the same page, PDF, slide deck, or email attachment:

- contributor weights, hours, `p`, `f`, or a contributor ceiling, **and**
- investor terms, `r_I`, `k_I`, the lid, or the split formula `θ`.

Hardware and equipment are not documented effort. They do not belong on page A as “we will credit your tools.” That cash, if it ever exists, is the [bond path](../capped-participating-bond/README.md#81-hardware-for-experiments). The soft-launch does not take that cash.

## 3. Banned on every public or promotional surface

Full “never” list: [annex-terms.md section F](annex-terms.md). Also banned on the website, a talk-track, and a trust-group note:

- Any return, yield, ROI, 10×, `r`, `k`, interest, *rente*, *rendement*, “outstanding”, “owed”, “balance”, “what your credits are worth.”
- A unit value of `p`, remaining euros per point, or a dashboard of a personal ceiling.
- “You earn more if you wait,” “will be worth more,” or a default multiple on a short payback.
- That credits become shares, or that share count will follow hours or points.
- That work now buys a later investment instrument, or that unpaid work will be converted into a bond.
- Investor terms (`r_I`, `k_I`, the lid, `θ`) on the contributor page.
- “Join and you will be paid,” or any wording a bookkeeper would book as a receivable of the contributor or a liability of the CV.
- Mapping hours to future share counts, even in a side spreadsheet “until we put it on paper.”

Preferred words: *ceiling / plafond*, *opening*, *unrecovered documented effort*, *contingent success fee*, *contribution weights*.

## 4. Contributor page — what is safe to say

- Refaqt’s mission. QOLAB is an instrument of that mission, not a private-profit scheme ([mission.md](../../../docs/mission.md)).
- Terms are **not final**. Counsel has not drafted the Dutch annex. Nothing on the page is an offer or a promise to pay.
- Accepted work is logged.
- Compensation, **if any**, is a **contingent success fee**: you invoice only if, in a later year, the cooperative opens a surplus pool after reserves and mission investment. In a year with no pool, nothing is due.
- The split among contributors is by **contribution weights** from accepted hours. Weights are not shares, not transferable, and will not be converted into shares.
- There is a **ceiling** tied to documented effort so nobody invoices without limit. Do not state the formula, `r`, `k`, a multiple, or a wait story.
- Governance membership, if it exists, is a **separate small cash share**, the same for each contributing member. Contribution is an eligibility gate, not a printer of extra paper.
- Staff-like people are employees on euro payroll. Contributors are independent (own clients, no subordination), typically through a company.

### Draft copy (ready to paste)

> **How contribution works (draft — terms not final)**
>
> Refaqt’s mission is to make manufacturing use less resources and energy by making it more decentralized and local, more on-demand, and by opening access to manufacturing and manufacturing tools.
>
> QOLAB is how we intend to record work on Refaqt projects. The Dutch contract is not drafted yet. Parameters can still change. This page is not an offer and not a promise to pay.
>
> When we accept work, we log it. If later the cooperative has a surplus after reserves and mission investment, independent contributors may invoice a contingent success fee. In a year with no surplus pool, nothing is due. A logged contribution may never be invoiced.
>
> The split among contributors uses contribution weights from accepted hours. Those weights are not shares. They are not transferable. They will not be converted into shares.
>
> Invoices, if they happen, stay under a ceiling tied to documented effort. Until counsel sets the parameters, a log of accepted work is a record of contribution, not a claim and not money owed.
>
> If you become a member, that is a separate small cash share for governance. It is the same for each contributing member. It is not extra paper for extra hours.
>
> People who work like staff are employees, paid in euros. Contributors are independent. They have other clients. They usually invoice through their own company.
>
> Counsel will write the Dutch terms. Until then, logged work is a record of accepted contribution, not a receivable.

## 5. Capital page — optional, not open

Publish this only if you need to say that capital exists as a **separate** path. Do not collect cash. Do not state rates or a multiple. Do not explain how contributors are paid on this page.

Safe to say:

- Money in would be a **separate** subordinated, contingent instrument, not shares-for-work.
- Repayment may never happen.
- After both labour and capital claims are capped, the residual belongs to the mission.
- **This instrument is not open. This page is not an offer.**

## 6. Talk-track (before someone joins)

Use the contributor page as the script. In conversation or a **private** one-pager (still no numbers), you may add:

- **Company.** For a Belgian person, this fee is heavily taxed. The design expects a contributor company. See the [adoption constraint](../../README.md#adoption-constraint).
- **Independence.** They have other clients. Refaqt does not direct them like staff.
- **Change.** The annex will be one standard document. The general assembly may amend it on one member, one vote. A reduction applies to **future opening** only. A ceiling already opened is honoured ([annex clause 13](annex-terms.md)).
- **Exit, in outline.** If they leave, any later ceiling that was already opened can still be invoiced if a pool exists. Exclusion for cause drops the row. Do not use the banned words in section 3.

Do not walk them through worked multiples, “expected 1×”, or the investor waterfall. Those belong in counsel packs, not recruiting.

## 7. Trust group — fact ledger and a short note

The [2026-09-10 start-off](../../../docs/log/2026-09-10_no-investors-euro-shares.md) is the test: **fact ledger + later invoices**, not informal profit shares.

### Ledger columns (one row per accepted item)

A spreadsheet is enough. Off-chain. Default stack is still not chosen.

| Column | What |
| --- | --- |
| person | Who did the work |
| project | Which Refaqt project |
| description | What was accepted |
| hours | Hours of accepted work |
| accept_date | When the work was accepted |
| accepted_by | Who accepted it |
| band_or_f | Frozen band, **or** quoted documented effort `f` |
| c | `hours × 100` (weight scale, not a euro rate) |

Optional: mint `p` only if the mint curve is written down as “current, may change for *future* accepts.”

Do **not** store or show: remaining euros, `rem / p`, a personal cap, a pool forecast, a share count, or an investor amount. Keep years with no pool (`P_t = 0`) as first-class records ([annex clause 22](annex-terms.md)). Equipment cost does not go in this ledger.

Until there is a real pool, **nobody invoices**. When there is, same fiscal year, amounts VAT-exclusive, default-in — that is when option 2 actually starts. The trust test is the ledger and the independence facts, not a handshake dividend.

### Short contractor note (checklist)

English is fine until counsel drafts Dutch. This is **not** an MoU for shares. It should say:

- Independent supply. IP / contributor licence as you already use.
- Logged `c` / `f` are for a **future contingent success fee** under option 2. They are not a fee earned, not a receivable, not “what you would have invoiced.”
- Nothing is due unless a pool opens. A row may never be invoiced.
- Weights will not become shares.
- The final annex is counsel’s. They accept that future-opening parameters may change.
- Instruction to the accountant: **do not book a ceiling** ([annex clause 27](annex-terms.md)).

Do not: map hours to future share counts; net later cash against logged work as if it were a loan; take investment money “on trust”; put both legs in the note.
