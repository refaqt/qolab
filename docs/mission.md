# Refaqt mission

Refaqt’s mission is to make manufacturing use less resources and energy by making it more decentralized and local, more on-demand, and by democratizing access to manufacturing and manufacturing tools.

QOLAB is an **instrument** of that mission: a contribution-credit and governance system for work on Refaqt projects. It is not a separate profit purpose.

## How to use this text

Token-system, statute, DVB, and contract drafts must be **checkable against** this mission. Reject sketches whose only story is private profit extraction or a tax result.

The mission is the candidate **non-tax rationale** for company form and surplus policy (art. 6:1 WVV *coöperatief doel* / *voorwerp*; DVB item **(q)**). It is **not** a tax box, a volunteer claim, or a ruling. FPS will read the shape of the scheme and whether operations match; a README does not make payments non-taxable.

Dutch statutory wording is **counsel**, not drafted here.

Recognition as *erkend als sociale onderneming* is **not sought** ([ADR 2026-09-15](decisions/2026-09-15_votes-off-the-reward.md)): the asset lock and the mission lock are written into the statutes and entrenched by a veto share, so art. 8:5 WVV’s ~6% cap is not a constraint. Whether to seek Nationale Raad voor de Coöperatie recognition is a separate matter, not decided here. Recording the mission is not a label.

Labour-quota shares remain a **dead-end**. The mission does not revive option 1, mix a dividend story and a fee on the same instrument, or convert contributors into volunteers.

Binding constraint: [ADR 2026-09-14](decisions/2026-09-14_refaqt-mission-design-constraint.md).
