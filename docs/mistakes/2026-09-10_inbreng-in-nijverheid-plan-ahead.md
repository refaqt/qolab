# 2026-09-10 — Inbreng in nijverheid needs a plan-ahead commitment

## What happened

Tried to use *inbreng in nijverheid* (art. 1:8 WVV) so contributors get shares for work without a bedrijfsrevisor on every task: bless hours × rate once, or send a list of hours and shares after the work, so people can be rewarded **later** without planning who will contribute, at which rate, and for how many hours.

## Why it went wrong

The revisor attaches to **each issuance** of shares against a labour / non-cash contribution, **before** those shares exist. He examines the board’s description and valuation of **this** contribution — a **promised** commitment, not a timesheet after the fact. A standing method is not a licence. A list of already-issued shares is the wrong order. You can batch a year, but that batch still needs names, bounded commitments, and rates. QOLAB’s product (reward later, no plan-ahead) is the opposite sequence. Hours × rate also prices the shares at grant.

## Prevention rule

Do not use *inbreng in nijverheid* (or a one-time method blessing, or a post-hoc hours list to the revisor) to unlock shares for work that was not committed and valued **before** issuance. If the reward is meant to come after unplanned work, keep labour off the share register: contingent fee / credits, or cash-issued shares whose number is **not** `f(hours)`. Do not issue extra profit-sharing shares whose number is set by contribution points.
